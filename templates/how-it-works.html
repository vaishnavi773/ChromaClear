How It Works

A deep-dive into the pipeline behind ChromaClear — from upload to download.

The Four-Step Pipeline

01
📸

Upload

Drop, browse or paste any greyscale or degraded photograph. Supported formats: JPEG, PNG, WEBP, BMP, TIFF — up to 16 MB. Images larger than 1024px are automatically resized to keep processing fast.

02
🧠

Colorize

The Zhang et al. deep convolutional network converts the image to CIELAB colour space, predicts the most plausible ab chrominance channels, then merges them with the original luminance for a full-colour result.

03
🔬

Enhance

Bilateral filtering denoises while preserving edges. Lanczos upsampling doubles or triples resolution. An unsharp mask recovers micro-detail lost to age or compression, followed by a perceptual clarity pass.

04
🎛️

Adjust & Download

Fine-tune brightness, contrast, saturation, warmth, sharpness and vignette using real-time CSS-filter sliders. Drag the before/after comparison slider, then export your result at full JPEG quality.

Colorization

Neural colour intelligence

ChromaClear uses the Zhang et al. (2016) deep convolutional network trained on millions of ImageNet photographs. The model predicts the most plausible ab chrominance channels in the CIELAB colour space — producing results that respect scene semantics: grass is green, skies are blue, skin tones are warm.

When the pre-trained Caffe weights are not installed, the algorithmic engine uses luminance-guided warm mapping to produce a tasteful tonal result — no model required.

Enhancement

Super-resolution & clarity

The auto-enhancement module runs a four-stage pipeline. OpenCV bilateral filtering suppresses noise while faithfully preserving edges. Then Lanczos-4 upsampling doubles or triples the resolution — far superior to nearest-neighbour or bilinear methods.

An unsharp mask then restores micro-detail lost to compression or age, followed by a final perceptual clarity pass to boost contrast and colour fidelity.

ChromaClear accepts PNG, JPEG, WEBP, BMP and TIFF files up to 16 MB. Images larger than 1024 pixels on either axis are automatically downscaled before processing to keep response times fast. Results are exported as high-quality JPEG.

The 1×, 2× and 3× buttons control the upscaling factor applied during the Enhancement step. A 2× upscale doubles both the width and height of the output image using Lanczos-4 interpolation paired with unsharp masking to maintain sharpness. Note that large scale factors on large input images can significantly increase processing time and output file size.
